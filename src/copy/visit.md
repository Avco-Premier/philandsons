## Seating and groups

We take reservations; call (718) 961-0263. Good for groups and kids.

## Ordering ahead and delivery

[Order ahead online](https://philandsonsonmain.com/order-now) and pick up at the restaurant. We deliver within about 2 miles to Queens, Flushing Chinatown, Murray Hill and Pomonok, with a $15 minimum. [Order online](https://philandsonsonmain.com/order-now) or call (718) 961-0263.

## Amenities and payment

You'll find a wheelchair-accessible entrance and free parking. We don't have outdoor seating, and dogs aren't allowed. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.

## What to order

**Meat Lovers Pizza (Large)**: The Meat Lovers Pizza (Large) costs $31.50. It's made with pepperoni, sausage and meatball &amp; mozzarella.

**Fried Calamari**: The Fried Calamari costs $14.20. It's made with lightly breaded; fried to golden perfection.

## About us

Phil & Sons on Main Italian restaurant and pizzeria is a pizza restaurant at 57-29 Main St in Queens, in Flushing, NY. Look for us in Queensboro Hill, beside Chase Bank and Yeh's Bakery, near NewYork-Presbyterian Queens Hospital. [Read our story](/about/).
