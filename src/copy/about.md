Phil & Sons on Main Italian restaurant and pizzeria is a pizza restaurant at 57-29 Main St in Queens, in Flushing, NY. Look for us in Queensboro Hill, beside Chase Bank and Yeh's Bakery, near NewYork-Presbyterian Queens Hospital. We're open for lunch and dinner, seven days a week.

The pizza list runs 58 choices long, from the Cheese Neapolitan Pizza to the Pizzeta Personal Pizza. There's also a pizza night special list with one choices. The Meat Lovers Pizza (Large) ($31.50) is made with pepperoni, sausage, meatball &amp; mozzarella. The Fried Calamari ($14.20) is made with lightly breaded; fried to golden perfection. For guests who avoid gluten or skip meat, the menu has the Eggplant Lasagna ( Vegetarian ), the Cauliflower Gluten Free Pizza and the Vegan Sicilian Pie (Large).

Guests rate Phil & Sons on Main Italian restaurant and pizzeria 4.3 stars across 602+ Google reviews. “Great people working here, suppose Sal is a good guy too.” (Fulton Hou, Google review) “A nice quick meal if you are visiting NYP Hospital.” (Kevin Megnauth, Google review).

You can eat in, take out or order delivery. You can order ahead online and pick it up at the restaurant. We deliver within about 2 miles with a $15 minimum. There's a free parking lot. Come see us at 57-29 Main St in Queens, or call (718) 961-0263 to order.

## Before you visit

You'll find a wheelchair-accessible entrance and free parking. We don't have outdoor seating, and dogs aren't allowed. We accept credit cards, debit cards and tap to pay.
